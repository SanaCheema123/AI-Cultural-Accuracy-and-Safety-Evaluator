I’m excited to share my latest AI project: CulturalGuard AI — AI Cultural Accuracy and Safety Evaluator.

CulturalGuard AI is designed to evaluate AI-generated images against Arabic prompts and provide structured insights around:

Prompt–image similarity
Cultural accuracy and contextual consistency
Safety and inappropriate-content detection
Bias and stereotype indicators
Missing or inconsistent visual elements
Human-review recommendations
Historical evaluation tracking

The platform is built with a modern full-stack architecture using React, TypeScript, Vite, FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL/SQLite, and Google Gemini integration, with support for Arabic/English language switching and RTL layouts.

A major focus of this project was building it as more than just a static dashboard. The application includes a complete workflow from image upload and Arabic prompt input to AI analysis, structured findings, confidence signals, and evaluation history.

I also focused heavily on:

Clean and responsive SaaS UI/UX
Secure authentication and JWT session handling
Live AI evaluation instead of fabricated demo scores
Arabic-first usability and RTL responsiveness
Explainable results with evidence, confidence, and recommendations
Modular architecture for future AI model integration

This project has been a great opportunity to work across AI/ML, full-stack development, SaaS architecture, UX design, authentication, API integration, and production-oriented engineering.

Still improving the system with better evaluation models, human expert review workflows, richer analytics, and stronger benchmarking capabilities.
